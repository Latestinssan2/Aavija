Designed for Pure Speed & Privacy.

No accounts, no Bluetooth pairing headaches, and no telemetry. Just native code running straight on your hardware.

✊

Natural Mid-Air Gestures

Vision on macOS and MediaPipe on Android track hand landmarks at 10 fps. Close your fist to GRAB, open to RELEASE. Frames are never recorded or sent.

🖥️

Screen & Active App Sharing

Powered by Apple's modern ScreenCaptureKit. Grab your entire 5K display or isolate the frontmost active application window without stray files on disk.

🪄

Magic Blackout Screen

Turns the Android screen 100% OLED black and hides the status bar for video magic tricks, while the background gesture camera remains active.

🔍

Edge-to-Edge Viewer

Zero borders, zero unwanted glow cards. Fluid multi-touch pinch to zoom (1x to 5x), pan while zoomed, and swipe down to dismiss.

🔒

Zero-Trust TLS 1.3

6-digit PIN challenge-response, certificate SHA-256 fingerprint pinning, path-traversal rejection, and a strict 200 MB transfer cap.

⚡

100% Native Architecture

Pure Swift + SwiftUI for macOS, and native Kotlin + Jetpack Compose for Android. No Electron, no Flutter, and no background battery drain.

How The Gesture Bridge Works

Cross-device handshake orchestrated via mDNS and encrypted sockets.

1

Bonjour Discovery

Android advertises via NsdManager. Mac browses and pairs via 6-digit PIN.

2

Mac GRAB ✊

Vision senses closed fist. ScreenCaptureKit captures display or active app into memory.

3

Phone RELEASE 🖐

Hand opens in front of phone. Matched within 10s token-gated window.

4
