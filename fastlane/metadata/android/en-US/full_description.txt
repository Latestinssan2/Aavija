Aavija is a native hand-gesture file and screenshot sharing app between Android phones and macOS/Windows over local Wi-Fi.

Features:
- Hand gesture sharing: GRAB to hold, RELEASE to send within 10 seconds.
- Screen and active app screenshot sharing.
- End-to-end TLS 1.3 encryption with 6-digit PIN pairing.
- No cloud, no tracking, no data leaves your network.
- Magic blackout screen mode for video demos.
- Background gesture scanning via foreground service.

Works on Android 10+.
